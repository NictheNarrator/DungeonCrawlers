# Verification

## September 18, 2026 — survivor relationships build

Verified on the recovered working copy, then committed and pushed as `be7a10b`.

- All 184 automated checks pass (`npm test`). They cover room transitions and prop reachability, the peaceful no-NPC route, worst-case ratman balance and death, turn control, defend, healing, fleeing, and invalid actions, then the full survivor matrix for Mara, Tobin and Vex: help, friendship, one-time rewards, remembered assistance, credible and exposed lies, pickpocketing, discovery on room exit, betrayal that helpful actions cannot erase, finite trade stock, payments entering the seller's wallet, nonlethal defeat, looting, waking, execution, and no duplicate rewards.
- The d20 rules checks pass for stealth, sleight of hand (including the worked example), persuasion, intimidation, perception, saving throws, advantage, disadvantage, and for ability scores surviving a save while old saves gain the defaults.
- Witness checks pass: a theft, an attack and a killing seen by an onlooker are remembered and change that onlooker's attitude, a witnessed helpful action warms them, an unwitnessed theft leaves no trace outside the victim, and witnessed knowledge survives a save round-trip.
- Cross-survivor checks pass: threats change attitudes and Vex fights back, open robbery is surrendered to by Mara and Tobin while Vex resists, Vex reacts to an earlier robbery or killing of Tobin, and exposed lies and failed thefts have lasting outcomes.
- Playthrough checks pass: a full helpful run ends with three allies, information rewards and stronger attacks; a manipulative run ends with successful thefts, exposed reputation and betrayal; a violent run ends with all three dead, empty inventories and a still-usable exit. The three completed saves produce noticeably different end states.
- Save integrity passes: version 1 saves migrate attitudes, deaths and inventories without reviving anyone, malformed saves fail safely, and unconscious and completed saves round-trip.
- Input checks pass: held direction repeats, release stops, a click does not double-step, cancelled touches and app pause stop movement, and only the active finger owns direction.
- Sprite checks pass: the renderer runs for real against a stub DOM and every world object — each prop, loot item, survivor and creature — must draw its own unique sprite rather than the generic fallback, and no sprite may call itself.
- Item-icon checks pass: every pack entry, equipment row and reward result draws a chip whose silhouette is the item type and whose frame is its rarity, while plain numbers get no chip at all.
- Marker checks pass: the eight map categories (person, enemy, loot, door, safe, stairs, objective, prop) each draw a distinct 16px shape, the objective becomes ordinary loot once the errand is settled, a neutral survivor reads as a person while a hostile or downed one reads as a threat, and an item is never mistaken for a map category.
- Animation checks pass: the world keeps drawing while the player stands still, idle frames are throttled to a slow tick rather than screen rate, the loop rests whenever a dialog is open or the run has ended, and rendering rooms at two different ticks changes only the living things and the brazier — nothing else.
- Floor layout checks pass: every doorway leads somewhere real, lands on a walkable tile, can be walked back the way you came, and never drops you inside furniture; the stairwell is reachable on foot from the entrance; characters rehome themselves clear of doorways instead of walling a route off; and routines, noise and "away from the danger" all use the doorway graph rather than room numbers.
- Access Control checks pass: Tobin's maintenance badge lives in exactly one inventory at a time and can be talked out of him, bought, lifted, robbed, or taken off his unconscious or dead body, and never reappears where it came from; the security gate refuses passage and says so, the maintenance route still reaches Records the long way round, showing the badge opens the short way, and handing it back to the ratmen buys their goodwill.
- Vex's route checks pass: taking his deal brings him along as a temporary ally, cutting the security feed in the Electrical Control Room needs him nearby or a real Investigation check, the shutdown opens the gate without a badge, the security cache pays out coins and a signet, honouring the deal settles it and letting him pick first, and keeping it all is remembered as a betrayal with his standing moved.
- Survivor checks pass: Mara names Eli and June and where they were last seen; Eli is walled into Records and comes out when the barricade is shifted, or stays put when the attempt fails; June cannot walk out untreated, a bandage fixes that, and both reach the safe room and stay saved through a reload. Ignoring June after being told about her lets her die while you rest, while a crawler who never heard of her is not blamed. Both can be threatened and both can be killed and stay dead.
- Sump Maw checks pass: it is a real creature in the food store with its own stat block, it can be fought and killed and stays dead, lured out of the store with food that is actually spent, shut in behind the maintenance door on a successful Investigation check and left loose on a failed one, or handed to the brute, who wins and pays for it. Every solution leaves the ratmen grateful and moves their standing.
- Sandbox checks pass: the exact combination the design asks for - Tobin dead, his badge taken off the body, Skrit alive and helped, the ratmen still distrusting you, Vex recruited, June in the safe room and Eli dead - all hold at once and survive a save, and a dead owner's badge still opens the gate. Helping a ratman while robbing a survivor leaves each opinion where it belongs, and standing stays per person rather than per faction.
- Inspector checks pass: it is neutral, it talks, and walking up to it starts nothing, but the stairwell is shut until you are authorised and the checkpoint says so. Forcing past it or fighting it is a real choice with real consequences. The Exit Authorization Stamp in the security cache gets you through, and so does quoting the regulation from the memo while carrying the maintenance badge - a Persuasion check that can fail, and that cannot even be attempted without the credentials. Vex's shutdown opens the checkpoint as well, the ratmen's gratitude reveals a tunnel route that skips it entirely, the suspended platform kills it outright and leaves the stairwell unguarded, and it can be killed in a straight fight and stays dead. The ratmen can also be sent at it, which hurts it badly and makes it hostile.
- Floor completion checks pass: reaching the stairwell with a key ends the floor by name, with the Dungeon AI line and a Floor 2 stub rather than a cliff; the clock stops and the level stops taking input; a finished run saves as finished with its ending intact; and the outcome summary covers the time left, the level and XP, who is still breathing and who is not, both faction standings, what the player actually did down here, the companion epilogue and the lessons learned. Two very different playthroughs produce two different summaries, so the ending describes the run rather than a script.
- Conversation checks pass: every line Mara, Skrit, Tobin, Vex, Eli and June can be answered with is a spoken sentence rather than a menu verb, each one is filtered through the engine's own action list so a line can never drift away from what the game can actually do, and skill checks carry a small standard tag (Persuasion, Deception, Intimidation, Insight, Athletics, Medicine) with the line itself as the main text. Driving the scene the way a player does - open the conversation, say the first line - runs the real action underneath: Mara takes the bandage and remembers the help. The world card keeps Inspect, Talk, Trade, Pickpocket, Rob and the combat verbs, and the conversation verbs are no longer listed on it.
- Portrait checks pass: the drawn set is built from one shared renderer so the cast shares a crop, a light and a palette, with the six faces differing in skull width, jaw, chin, eye spacing and colour, nose, mouth, hair and gear. all six named characters have their own portrait entry, no two share a file, each carries a drawn fallback, a fallback file for the set and a slot for expression variants later, a creature with no portrait is left to its sprite, and opening a conversation with one character after another swaps the portrait with them rather than leaving the last one on screen.
- Phase 1 of the Watercolor Pixel overhaul passes: the sixteen-colour palette was sampled off the approved guide's printed strip rather than guessed and lives in src/palette.mjs as the single source of truth, with the stylesheet mirroring every swatch and the renderer's room table drawing only from it; the twelve Floor 1 rooms each have a floor pair and an accent wall band; the world cell is the guide's 16px base at 4x, the portrait canvas is exactly the drawn grid at a whole multiple, nothing ever turns smoothing on, every image-scaling canvas has smoothing off first, the zoom lands on whole device pixels, and canvases, item chips and map markers are all displayed nearest-neighbour.
- The static build passes (`npm run build`) and every runtime file, including `src/npcs.mjs`, is served over HTTP with the expected contents by the local preview server.

Not verified in this pass: no browser click-through of the NPC tabs, relationship panel, or completion summary was performed, and no physical phone testing was done.
